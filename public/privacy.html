Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 August 2026 · Applies to app.fishwizz.com and the FishWizz mobile apps

FishWizz is a personal fishing log and planning tool. This policy explains exactly what it collects, why, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version. Your catches, gear, spots, and locations are yours. They are stored under your account, protected so that no other user can read them, and never sold. Precise location and photos are collected only when you actively use a feature that needs them. You can delete everything permanently from inside the app.

1. Who we are

FishWizz ("we", "us") operates the FishWizz web application and mobile apps. For privacy questions, data requests, or complaints, contact privacy@fishwizz.com.

What we do not collect

  • We do not run background or continuous location tracking. Location is read only while you are using a feature that needs it.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party advertising cookies.
  • We do not collect contacts, calendar, microphone, or health data.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

3. Legal bases (UK/EU users)

Where GDPR applies, we process your data to perform our contract with you (running your account and storing your fishing data), on the basis of your consent (device location and camera access, which you may withdraw at any time in your browser or OS settings), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and functioning.

6. Your rights and controls

  • Delete everything. Open My FishWizz → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, catches, gear, spots, sessions, and photos. It cannot be undone.
  • Export your data. My FishWizz → Export downloads your data as a JSON file.
  • Revoke location or camera access at any time in your browser or device settings. The rest of the app keeps working.
  • Access, correct, restrict, or object. Edit your records in the app, or email privacy@fishwizz.com.
  • If you are in the UK/EU you may complain to your local supervisory authority. If you are in California, you may exercise CCPA/CPRA rights without being discriminated against for doing so.

7. Security

Every request runs over HTTPS. Your rows are protected by database row-level security policies that scope every read and write to your own user id, enforced by the database itself rather than by the app — so another signed-in user cannot reach your data even by calling the API directly. Uploaded photos live in private storage buckets scoped to your user id and are served only through short-lived signed links.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant regulator as required by law.

8. Children

FishWizz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we make a material change we will update the date above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
